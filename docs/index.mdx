---
title: "IBC"
description: "Packets between chains, verified by on-chain light clients. The protocol, its Solidity contracts, the IBC CLI, and the Go implementation."
---

IBC moves packets between chains and proves, on each side, that what arrived is
what was sent. This site covers the protocol, the Solidity contracts that run it
on a chain, the CLI that deploys and operates it, the Go implementation, and the
specifications all of them follow.
